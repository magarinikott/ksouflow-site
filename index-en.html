the future is not set in stone

KSOU FLOW does not deliver verdicts or claim to know your fate. A reading can help you notice the emotional dynamics of the present, name hidden tension, and make room for your own decision.

the essentials

questions without the fog

does it predict the future?

no. the app helps you reflect on the current dynamics of a situation without promises or fatalistic language.

do I need to know Lenormand?

no. the interface and interpretations require no specialist knowledge.

what can I ask about?

relationships, work, choices, and situations where you want greater inner clarity.

how are my readings stored?

access to your history is protected by authentication. Service providers process data to create and store readings. See our privacy policy for details.

should I rely on KSOU FLOW for important decisions?

no. KSOU FLOW is a reflection tool, not a substitute for medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice. Your decisions remain your own.
