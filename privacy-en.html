privacy policy

this policy explains how the KSOU FLOW app collects, uses, stores, and deletes user data. The KSOU FLOW developer is the app operator and the privacy point of contact.

data we process

  • email address, account identifier, and session data used for registration, sign-in, and account recovery;
  • name, birth date and time, birth city, and current city, only when provided voluntarily;
  • question text, selected cards, interpretations, follow-up questions, and reading history;
  • support requests and information included in them;
  • technical request, error, app-version, and device information required for security and diagnostics.

how we use data

  • to create and maintain your account;
  • to generate readings and answers;
  • to save your history and settings;
  • to provide support, prevent abuse, and fix errors;
  • to protect the security and stability of the service.

service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage services;
  • Cloudflare Workers processes server requests and helps protect the service;
  • OpenAI and/or DeepSeek generates written interpretations. The question text and reading context required to produce an answer may be sent to these providers.

providers receive only the data required for their function and process it under their own terms and privacy policies.

advertising and payments

the current KSOU FLOW version contains no advertising, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not accept payments. This policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that changes.

retention and security

account data and history are retained while the account is active or as needed to provide requested features. Data is transmitted over secure connections, and server access is restricted through authentication and permissions.

infrastructure providers may temporarily retain technical logs for security and diagnostics. We do not sell personal data.

account and data deletion

delete your account in the app under profile → delete account, or use the account deletion page.

deletion removes the account, profile, questions, readings, history, and related support requests. To prevent repeated signup bonuses, an irreversible hash of the email may be retained without the address, name, or history. Backup and security logs expire under the relevant provider’s retention cycle.

rights and contact

for access, correction, or deletion requests, contact hello@ksouflow.tech. Never send your password or sign-in codes.
