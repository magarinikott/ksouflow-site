manage your data

account deletion

KSOU FLOW users can delete their account and associated data in the app or submit a request on this page.

In the current closed test, in-app deletion works for accounts without purchases. Purchases and automatic deletion of paid accounts are not enabled yet; the subscription flow below applies to the first public version. If you need help deleting test data, contact hello@ksouflow.tech.

delete in the app

Open profile → delete account and confirm. With an active subscription, you may turn off renewal and keep access through the paid period or delete now after a separate warning about lost access. Deletion does not itself refund a payment. If RuStore cannot confirm cancellation, in-app deletion remains available after a warning about possible further charges.

request deletion without the app

email us from the address registered to the account. We may ask you to confirm ownership, but we will never ask for your password or sign-in code.

send deletion request

requests are processed within 30 days after account ownership is confirmed.

data deleted

  • account and profile, including voluntarily provided profile data;
  • questions, readings, and history;
  • balance, settings and referral data linked to the profile.

A protected email-derived marker remains up to 12 months to prevent repeat welcome and promotional bonuses. Minimal operational purchase data may remain separately from the deleted profile for no more than 24 months after the paid period, refund or dispute ends; an open case is reviewed until resolved. Required tax documents are archived separately for the applicable statutory period. REG.RU support-mail copies are removed six months after the last correspondence unless a dispute is open. Existing backups expire under the configured cycle. See the privacy policy for details.
